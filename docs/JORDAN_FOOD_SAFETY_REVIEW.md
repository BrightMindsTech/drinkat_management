**Operating country:** Jordan.  
**Internal baseline:** FDA Food Code 2026 (U.S. model code).  
**Precedence:** Applicable Jordanian law and JFDA instruments take precedence over the FDA model code.

## What this means for DRINKAT QC

FDA Food Code 2026 may be used as the **internal** reference for handwashing, TCS holding, date marking, allergen notice, and food-contact cleaning **as DRINKAT house guidance**, labeled as FDA.

It does **not** automatically satisfy:

- JFDA licensing / municipal licensing of the branch
- Jordanian technical regulations (JS / JFDA health measures)
- Worker health-card or medical-exam rules that JFDA or the municipality may require (specific current instrument not quoted here — QC must obtain the applicable text)
- Immediate-closure criteria published by JFDA (2025-04-02 guide — retrieve the PDF before writing a DRINKAT stop-sale rule)
- Any Amman / local municipality restaurant conditions

## Differences requiring QC / owner review (no invented thresholds)

1. **Competent authority:** Jordan = JFDA (+ municipality). FDA = U.S. model for adopting jurisdictions.  
2. **Licensing:** Jordan requires JFDA (and often municipal) approval to operate a food establishment. The FDA Food Code does not issue a Jordan license.  
3. **Circulation definition:** Jordanian Food Law covers production through sale/gift. Map DRINKAT bar + kitchen + storage to that scope.  
4. **Unfit / spoiled food:** JFDA Director-General powers include seizure and destruction of specified perishable foods after physical inspection. Do not invent a DRINKAT destruction SOP from FDA 3-501.18 alone.  
5. **Closure:** JFDA publishes negatives that require immediate closure or suspension. FDA does not write Jordan closure triggers.  
6. **Allergen / labeling:** FDA 3-602.12(C) is the internal baseline. Jordanian packaging/labeling technical regulations must be checked before claiming legal allergen compliance.  
7. **Tourist restaurants / factories:** JFDA service text treats some categories differently. Confirm how each DRINKAT branch is classified before copying a license checklist.
